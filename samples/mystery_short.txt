TITLE: THE LAST STUDY ROOM
TYPE: SHORT FILM
GENRE: MYSTERY / PSYCHOLOGICAL THRILLER

INT. UNIVERSITY STUDY ROOM – MORNING

The room is empty except for a table and two chairs. MAYA enters with a notebook and places a rubber door stopper under the door.

MAYA
Finally. Quiet.

She sits and begins highlighting notes.

A TAP drips in the corner.

Maya looks up. The sink is dry.

She returns to her notes.

The door closes behind her.

Maya freezes. She looks at the stopper. It is still under the door.

She pulls the handle. Locked.

MAYA
Very funny.

No answer.

The second chair now faces her.

Maya steps back. Her breathing quickens. She opens her bag for her phone and notices a photograph of her parents tucked inside her notebook.

She looks at the chair again.

MAYA
No. I'm not running.

She walks toward it.

CLICK. The door unlocks by itself.

Maya stops. She looks at the open doorway, then at the chair.

She leaves slowly.

The empty room is still.

The chair turns toward the door.

CUT TO BLACK.
