TITLE: BAD JOKE
TYPE: SHORT FILM
GENRE: UNIVERSITY ROMANCE

EXT. UNIVERSITY LIBRARY – AFTERNOON

NILA sits on a bench scrolling through her phone. Her friend AISHA notices a new follow request.

AISHA
You know him?

NILA
No. Should I?

Aisha looks across the courtyard. ARUN is helping a first-year student carry a box of books.

AISHA
That's Arun. Film club.

Nila accepts the request but immediately locks her phone.

INT. UNIVERSITY LIBRARY – LATER

Nila searches for a book. Arun reaches for the same copy.

ARUN
You take it. I only need it to look intelligent.

Nila laughs before she can stop herself.

ARUN
Good. My worst joke worked.

NILA
That was your worst?

ARUN
I have a frightening range.

They smile.

EXT. LIBRARY – EVENING

Nila catches up with Arun.

NILA
Why did you follow me?

ARUN
Last week, everyone laughed at my presentation because they were being polite. You laughed at the one joke that actually failed.

NILA
So?

ARUN
It felt real.

Nila studies him for a second, then smiles.

NILA
Your joke is still bad.

They walk away together.
