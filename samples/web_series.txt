TITLE: FRAME BY FRAME
TYPE: WEB SERIES
GENRE: COMING-OF-AGE DRAMA
EPISODES: 3 SAMPLE EPISODES

EPISODE 1 — THE PITCH

INT. UNIVERSITY MEDIA LAB – DAY

DEV shows his friends MIRA and KAI a rough short film on a laptop. The sound is terrible, the edit unfinished, but Dev watches it like a masterpiece.

DEV
One day I'm directing a feature.

KAI
One day you are fixing this audio first.

They laugh.

INT. DEV'S HOUSE – NIGHT

Dev's father places a graduate job application in front of him.

FATHER
Cinema can be a hobby. This is your future.

DEV
What if cinema is my future?

The room goes quiet.

END EPISODE: Dev receives an email inviting student creators to pitch a web series.

EPISODE 2 — OLD FOOTAGE

INT. UNIVERSITY ARCHIVE ROOM – EVENING

Dev searches old hard drives for material. He finds footage of LENA, a former friend he has not spoken to in two years.

MIRA
Delete it if it hurts.

DEV
What if it belongs in the story?

MIRA
Then decide whether you're making a film or hiding inside one.

END EPISODE: Lena appears at the media lab doorway.

EPISODE 3 — ONE TAKE

EXT. CAMPUS ROOFTOP – SUNSET

Dev and Lena finally speak. Their conversation begins awkwardly and becomes honest. Dev admits that he turned their memories into scenes because he did not know how to face what happened.

LENA
Then stop directing the conversation.

Dev lowers the camera he has been holding.

Later, Dev enters the pitch room without his prepared video. He tells the panel he wants to make a series about young people choosing their own path while learning that real life cannot be edited into perfection.

END EPISODE: The panel asks, "When can you start?"
